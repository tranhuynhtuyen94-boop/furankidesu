🧠 The 2 Core Building Blocks Learn these first!

1. Right Trigger ("Sexy Move")

R U R' U' (or on Bottom-Down: R D R' D')

Why it works: It lifts the Right layer up, slides a piece in/out of the horizontal layer, and restores the Right layer. Repeating it swaps and twists corner pieces without wrecking the rest of your cube!

2. Left Trigger

L' U' L U

Why it works: Exact mirror image of the Right Trigger for your left hand. Pairing Right Trigger + Left Trigger inserts Middle Layer edges and cycles Last Layer corners.

🗺️ 7-Stage Beginner Roadmap No app needed once learned

Stage 1: White Cross (Top)

Goal: Match the 4 White edges with BOTH the White center AND the 4 side centers (Green, Red, Blue, Orange).
Secret: Move a White edge to the bottom Yellow layer first, spin D until its side color matches its center, then turn that face 180° (F2) to bring it up!

Stage 2: White Corners (First Layer)

Goal: Complete the entire White face and top ring.
Secret: Put a White corner on the bottom layer directly underneath its target home between its matching centers, then repeat R' D' R D (1, 3, or 5 times) until it locks in.

Stage 3: Middle Layer Edges

Goal: Solve the 4 middle-layer edges.
Secret: Find an edge on the bottom layer with NO Yellow. Line up its front sticker to match its center:
• Goes Right: D' R' D R D F D' F'
• Goes Left: D L D' L' D' F' D F
(Rule of thumb: Move bottom AWAY from target side, pull target slot down, tuck back, restore!)

Stage 4: Yellow Cross (Bottom)

Goal: Make a Yellow "+" on the bottom layer.
Secret: Progress from Dot → L-Shape → Line → Cross using:
F (D R D' R') F'

Stage 5: Align Yellow Cross Edges (Sune)

Goal: Make all 4 bottom edges match their side centers.
Secret: Rotate D until at least 2 edges match. Hold matching edges at Back & Right (or Front & Back) and run Sune:
R D R' D R D2 R'

Stage 6: Position Yellow Corners (Niklas)

Goal: Put all 4 bottom corners in their correct home spots (even if twisted).
Secret: Hold a correct corner at Front-Right-Bottom and cycle the other 3:
D R D' L' D R' D' L

Stage 7: Twist Final Corners!

Goal: Twist each incomplete bottom corner so Yellow faces Down.
Secret: Bring an untwisted corner to Front-Right-Bottom using D, repeat R U R' U' (2 or 4 times) until Yellow faces Down. CRITICAL: Never rotate the whole cube—only spin D to bring the next corner over, and the top layer will magically re-assemble at the end!
